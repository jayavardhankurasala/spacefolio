ABOUT ME

|

I'm Jayavardhan Kurasala, a Computer Science Engineering student who enjoys turning practical problems into clear, usable software. I work across web development, interface design, and programming fundamentals, and I learn best by building, testing, and refining ideas. My recent projects include MediKiosk, a healthcare intake concept, and a prototype for Kochi Metro train-induction planning. I'm interested in how thoughtful software can make everyday tasks more accessible and organized. I value collaboration, curiosity, and honest engineering: a prototype should communicate a concept, while a production system needs reliable data, security, testing, and validation. I'm continuing to strengthen my programming, full-stack development, and problem-solving skills by building digital experiences with a clear purpose.
